## 9.1 Theme catalogue

Themes are **content packs**: a stage (background + palette), 12–25 objects with tags, a music key + lead instrument, and 1–2 **signature mechanics** that the generator prefers for that theme. Themes are ordered into **absurdity tiers** so the game gets weirder as you progress:

| Tier | Feel | Available from |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Everyday** | Familiar places and things | L1 |
| **B — Characterful** | Everyday places owned by someone specific/strange | L20 |
| **C — Fantastical** | Fantasy, sci-fi, monsters | L56 |
| **D — Absurd** | Concepts, impossible objects, meta-jokes | L150 |

## 9.2 LiveOps framework

### Principle: events are reskins + one rule
An event never needs new gameplay code. It is assembled from:

| Element | Source |
|---|---|
| **Event theme pack** | New stage + 20–30 seasonal objects (art cost: ~2 artist-weeks) |
| **Event twist rule** | One existing mechanic *re-themed* (e.g. Halloween's "cursed candy" = radioactive) |
| **Event track** | 20–30 event levels (6–8 hand-made headline levels + generated filler) |
| **Event currency** | A themed token (pumpkins, snowflakes) earned per event level and per campaign level during the event |
| **Rewards** | Event-exclusive container skins and Museum "Seasonal Wing" objects |

### Event calendar & re-themed mechanics

| Event | Dates (approx.) | Re-themed mechanic | Headline level | Exclusive reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **New Year** | Dec 28 – Jan 4 | Fuses (fireworks) + sequence (countdown order 10→1) | "Clean up after the party before midnight… of next year" | Disco-ball containers |
| **Valentine's Day** | Feb 7 – Feb 15 | Pairs + sticky (sweethearts stick together) | "Sort the love letters (one is from a stalker — disguised)" | Heart-shaped bins with blushing faces |
| **Easter / Spring** | ~2 weeks around Easter | Fragile eggs + nested (eggs in eggs) + hidden eggs in the pile | "The Easter Bunny's Cluttered Burrow" | Bunny-ear containers |
| **Summer** | Jul 1 – Jul 21 | Perishable (melting ice cream) + floaty (beach balls), tilt (boat) | "Pack the beach bag before the tide comes in" | Inflatable flamingo bins |
| **Halloween** | Oct 20 – Nov 2 | Mimics, ghosts, lights out, "cursed candy" (radioactive) | "Trick-or-Treat Haul Audit" | Jack-o'-lantern containers |
| **Christmas** | Dec 10 – Dec 27 | Request queue (Santa's list), nested gifts, naughty/nice inferred rule | "Santa's Returns Desk" | Gift-box containers with bows |
| Lunar New Year | Late Jan/Feb | Sequence (zodiac order), red-envelope count rule | "Dragon parade clean-up" | Lantern bins |
| Back to School | Late Aug | Owner rule (whose stuff), shape slots (pencil cases) | "The Lost Property Mountain" | Backpack bins |

### Recurring event types (between seasonal moments)

| Type | Cadence | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekend Mess** | Every weekend | One huge Big Mess level with a leaderboard |
| **Mechanic Week** | Every 2 weeks | "Fragile Week": event levels emphasise one mechanic; badge for no-break streaks |
| **Theme Drop** | Monthly | A new theme pack enters the campaign rotation + a 5-level showcase |
| **Collab/Crossover** (later) | Quarterly | Brand-safe licensed object packs (e.g. a film's props) — business-development dependent |

**Production cadence (steady state):** 1 seasonal event / 6–8 weeks, 1 Theme Drop / month, 1 campaign chapter (15 levels) / 2 weeks via generator + curation.

## 9.3 Daily Sort

### Structure
- **One puzzle per day, the same for everyone globally** (seeded by UTC date, deterministic physics).
- Unlocks at **L20**.
- Two attempts count: the **first attempt** is the *Official* run (leaderboard + share card); unlimited *practice* runs afterwards (no leaderboard, boosters allowed).
- Length target: 45–75 s.

### Generation
- Generated 60 days ahead from a weekly difficulty template, validated with the full gauntlet (Ch. 05 §5.5), approved by a designer weekly.
- Only uses mechanics introduced by **L60** (so every eligible player knows them); a second "Daily Chaos" tier (unlocked L150) uses everything.

### Weekly difficulty rhythm

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | Medium | Medium | Hard | Medium | **Big Mess** (long) | Hard + twist |

### Scoring
`Score = 10,000 − (time_ms / 10) − (Oops × 500) + (max_combo × 50) + (no_break_bonus 300)`, floored at 0. Shown as time + mistakes on the share card (scores are opaque; times are understandable).

### Rewards
- Completion: 50 coins + 1 streak day.
- Percentile reward: top 50% +25 coins, top 10% +75 coins, top 1% "Golden Bin" badge for the day.
- Streak milestones: 3 days (booster pack), 7 days (Mystery crate with rare Oddity), 30 days (exclusive "Calendar" container skin), 100 days (animated skin).
- **Streak freeze:** 1 free per week; extra via rewarded ad. Losing a long streak is a top churn risk; freezes protect it.

### Leaderboards
- **Friends** (via platform game services / invite links), **Country**, **Global** (shown as percentile, e.g. "Faster than 87% of sorters"), with the top 100 viewable.
- Anti-cheat: inputs are recorded (deterministic replay); server re-simulates top-1,000 scores; impossible inputs rejected.

### Sharing (Wordle-style card)
```
SORT EVERYTHING — Daily #214 🧦
"The Wizard's Laundry"
⏱ 0:42   ❌ 0   🔥 combo ×9
🟩🟩🟩🟩🟨🟩🟩🟩   (one square per object batch: green=perfect, yellow=fixed mistake)
Faster than 87% of sorters
sorteverything.game/d/214
```
- Text-based so it pastes anywhere; link opens the store or (if installed) the daily.

### Replayability
- Daily archive (last 30 days) playable for coins (no leaderboard) — monetisable via rewarded unlock beyond the last 7 days.
- "Beat my ghost": on any past daily, race the replay ghost of your friend's best run.

## 9.4 Social & viral design

**Rule:** Social features must emerge from moments players already want to share. Nothing is gated behind friends.

| Feature | Natural moment | Mechanism | Launch? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fail clips** | A hilarious physics fail (explosion, egg avalanche, radioactive spread) | The game keeps a rolling 5-second input+physics replay buffer. On the fail screen: "Share this disaster" exports a 6-s vertical MP4 with the fail sting and a caption ("I was 1 object away 😭") | Soft launch |
| **Trick-shot clips** | Bank shot, mid-air save, ×10 combo | Auto-detected "highlight" moments; result screen offers "Share this throw" | Global launch |
| **Daily share card** | Daily Sort completion | Text card (above) | Soft launch |
| **Milestone cards** | L100, L200… | Image card: "I've sorted 4,812 things including 1 radioactive banana" | Launch |
| **Friend challenge** | After a good campaign level time | "Challenge a friend" link → friend plays the same level (no install needed on the web demo, Month 6+) and races your ghost | Month 2 |
| **"Impossible-looking" level clips** | Chaos levels | Marketing-side: these are UA creatives; in-game, the teaser card itself is shareable ("Next I have to sort *what*?") | Launch (teaser share) |
| **Museum flex** | Completing a wing / rare Oddity | Image card of the shelf | Month 1 |

**Not doing:** forced Facebook login, friend gifting of lives (no lives), guilds/teams (wrong fit for a 40-second puzzle loop; revisit for Month 12 only if social data supports it), chat.

### Why it can be viral
- **Fail clips are inherently watchable** (physics comedy + recognisable characters + 6 seconds).
- **The premise is a meme template:** "Sort Everything — now sort *X*." Players and creators can suggest X; the game can literally ship the community's best suggestions as Theme Drops ("Community Theme of the Month").
